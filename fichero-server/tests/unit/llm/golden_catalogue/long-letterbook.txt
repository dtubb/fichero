Letter book of the Hartley Mill Company, Leeds, covering January to December 1874, with 112 copied letters in English. The volume contains the manager's letters to wool suppliers in Bradford and Halifax, to the Great Northern Railway about delayed wagons, and to the insurers, Phoenix Fire Office. It records orders of 40 and 65 bales of wool in February, and states that the dye house closed from 9 to 23 July for repair of a boiler. A letter of 3 October 1874 to the mill's solicitor, Mr Thwaite, describes a dispute with a neighbour, Samuel Ogden, over a drain. Letters of 15 and 28 November name 22 workers dismissed after a fire in the carding room. Several entries carry [uncertain] readings of sums, kept as written. The last letter, dated 30 December 1874, thanks the Bradford Banking Company for an extension of credit of 1,200 pounds. An index of correspondents on the first four pages lists thirty-one names, and the volume is paginated by hand.
