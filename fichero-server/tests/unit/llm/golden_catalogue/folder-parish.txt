Folder of 58 documents from the parish of San Miguel, 1790-1815: 31 baptism entries, 19 marriage records and 8 burial entries, in Spanish with a few lines in Latin. It names the parish priests Fr. Anselmo Rojas and Fr. Tomas Vega and the places San Miguel, Santa Ana and the hacienda of La Palma. The baptisms record the parents' names, the godparents, and the child's status as the priest wrote it, including 'espanol', 'mestizo' and 'indio'. The marriage records state the ages and origins of the couples, and fourteen of them list witnesses. The burial entries give the cause of death in five cases, among them 'viruelas' in 1798 and 1799, and name the person who paid the fee. Entries from 1803 are in a different hand, and two pages are marked [ilegible] where the ink has run. The folder holds no index. Entries are in date order within each type, and the 1809 baptisms are missing between March and June, as the register itself states.
