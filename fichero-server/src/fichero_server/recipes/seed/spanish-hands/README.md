# Historical Spanish hands

The flagship recipe: letters, notarial and administrative records in Spanish hands, 1500-1900.
Lines are found and read on your Mac with Kraken; a local vision model checks only the lines the
reader is unsure of; names, dates and statements are found for the knowledge graph; the work is
kept as TEI and PAGE in a synced folder. When enough corrected lines exist, training a reader on
your own pages is offered.

The model pins are illustrative until the flagship's measurement sets them.
