## The two findings that matter most

**Language (#2092).** The pipeline assumed English four separate ways on an
archive that is largely Spanish: `en-US` hardcoded in TWO places (fixing one
would have left the other), the literal string `'auto'` shipped into a prompt
as if it were a language, NER never consulting the policy at all, and
`language_identification` **fabricating `'en'` on no signal**. That last is the
north star inverted — asked "what language is this?" with nothing to go on, it
answered "English" confidently. On this material every one of those produces
plausible-looking wrong output, which this project ranks above crashes.

**Enforcement (#3804).** The audit disproved its own issue. The premise was
"the menu offers workflows the engine cannot run"; in fact the menu is
generated from the API, so no entry can name a missing workflow. The real
defect is the inverse: `_workflow_direct_runnable()` is the ONLY reader of
`config.internal` in the entire server, feeding one field that one SwiftUI view
filters on. **The engine never re-checks at run time, so CLI, MCP, Shortcuts
and any direct POST walk straight past it.** A rule enforced in exactly one
caller is not enforced.
