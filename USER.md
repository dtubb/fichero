# About Daniel Tubb

Fichero is made by [Daniel Tubb](https://tubb.ca). Daniel is an anthropologist and an Associate Professor of Anthropology at the University of New Brunswick.

## Where Fichero comes from

Historians and anthropologists work with primary sources: scanned archives, field notes, interviews, transcripts, historical documents. Fichero is a tool to work with this research material. Daniel is the Creative Director of Fichero, and he steers development through issues, review, and day-to-day use. The project's development workflow is documented in the [FAQ](docs/user_manual/guide/Part I. Getting Started/4-frequently-asked-questions.md).

## Contributing and contact

Fichero is open source and built in the open. Issues and pull requests are
welcome on [GitHub](https://github.com/dtubb/fichero). For how the project is
developed, see [How Fichero Works](docs/contributor_manual/how-fichero-works.md). For setup and
conventions, see the [Contributor Guide](docs/contributor_manual/README.md). To use Fichero,
