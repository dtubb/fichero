# Fichero: AI for archives

Fichero is a Mac app to read archives and other research material;  to transcribe handwritten documents to generate structured data from the results using AI tools and workflows; and to navigate, search, and edit the results.

To learn more about how Fichero works, please read the [FAQ](user/guide/10-frequently-asked-questions.md).

**Fichero is open source, but it is in early days, and Fichero is in Public *Alpha*.**

## Features

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-folder-multiple: __Library__

    ---

    Documents are stored in a library, which can contain folders,
    images, pdfs, audio, video, and other documents. Browse them as lists, 
    icons, tables, and columns; or extract data and browse them as datasets, timelines, and boards; or organize them visually on a canvas.

-   :material-sitemap: __AI workflows__

    ---

    Use built-in AI tools and workflows (or chain both together) along with custom free local or cloud AI models, or make your own. 

-   :material-image: __Preview__

    ---

    The image, PDF, and document viewer, lets you view a document; the word
    boxes over the scan; draw and save regions; make highlight and annotations; flip between different renditions of a page.

-   :material-image-edit: __Edit__

    ---

    Rotate, crop, enhance, and remove backgrounds to make reading a document
    easier.

-   :material-book-open-variant: __Reader__

    ---

    Keep transcripts, translations, and other "artifacts" that you or AI
    write or generate, side by side with the original image.

-   :material-information: __Inspector__

    ---

    Edit metadata, artifacts, entities, annotations, edit
    history, and the knowledge graph, for each document.

-   :material-graph: __Knowledge__

    ---

    Automatically read names, places, and dates out of your documents and
    turns them into simple statements. (e.g. who did what, where, and when).
    Each statement is tied to the exact words on the page it came from.
    Fichero can then create a knowledge graph of your archive, which you can
    edit, or browse as biographies, maps, and timelines. Each "statement" is
    tied to its source text.

-   :material-magnify: __Search__

    ---

    Use keyword or semantic search powered by a powerful vector database
    to search across a library or a folder, and see the relevant text with
    matched words highlighted in the results.

</div>
