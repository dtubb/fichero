# Appendix D: Glossary

> 🤖 *AI Drafted (Not reviewed)*


**Library.** A .fichero package on disk holding your documents and everything Fichero knows about them. One window works with one library.

**Document.** Anything imported into the library: a PDF, a scan, a recording, a folder. Documents form a tree.

**Page.** One page of a document. Most extracted facts are anchored to a page.

**Rendition.** An alternative image of the same page — the archival original, an enhanced version. Flip with ↑/↓.

**Reader.** The center pane that shows the page, with tabs for the page itself, its extracted knowledge, and your notes.

**Inspector.** The right-hand panel: Source, Notes, Knowledge, and Artifacts sections for the selected document.

**Annotation.** A marking tied to a page, text span, or region. Can be revealed on the page and promoted into a claim.

**Note.** Free-text writing linked to a document as a whole, kept separate from the source text.

**Workflow.** A step-by-step processing pipeline (transcribe, extract, catalogue) run over selected documents.

**Preset.** A ready-made workflow that ships with Fichero. There are about fifty.

**Artifact.** Anything a workflow produces — a transcription, a catalogue entry, a dataset — attached to its source document.

**Entity.** A named thing extracted from your sources: a person, place, organization, event, concept, or date.

**Claim.** A statement extracted from a source — who did what, where, when — tied to the page it came from.

**Knowledge graph.** The web of entities and claims across the whole library, browsable and searchable.

**Curation.** Your review of extracted material: approve, reject, merge, or suppress entities and claims.

**Provenance.** The recorded origin of every extracted item: which document, page, workflow, and model produced it.

**Semantic search.** Search by meaning: results can match your query's sense even when the exact words differ.

**Engine.** The backend server inside Fichero that stores, searches, and processes your library.
